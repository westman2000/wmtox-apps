Privacy Policy — Retro LCD: Wolf & Eggs

Effective date: 25 October 2026. Applies to the Android phone and Wear OS versions of the game.

No account, no tracking

The game works without an account or an internet connection. The current version contains no advertising or analytics, does not request access to contacts, the camera, microphone or location, and does not send your data to the developer.

Optional support purchases

Settings include an optional way to support the author with a purchase through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; for this, the Google Play Billing library may use the network. The developer receives only anonymised purchase statistics from Google Play Console and never sees your payment details. The purchase unlocks nothing in the game; the device only stores a note that you supported the author.

Data stored on your device

The device stores sound, vibration and tutorial settings, an unfinished game and personal bests, including score, mode, game duration and result date. These records support resuming play and displaying personal bests. The phone and watch versions have separate saved data and do not automatically sync progress.

The vibration permission is used for game feedback, which can be disabled in settings.

System backup

The app allows Android system backup and device-to-device transfer. Depending on the device and backup service settings, the system may include game settings and saved progress in a backup or transfer them to another device. The developer does not operate this service or receive the contents of these backups. Backup settings and deletion are managed through the relevant system or backup provider.

Deleting your data

Local data is retained for the game to function. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. An incompatible change to the game rules may also remove previous scores and an unfinished game. Android may restore an existing system backup after reinstallation; management of that backup is separate.

Contact and changes

If you contact support, use the contact above and share only the information needed for your request. This policy will be updated on this page if data handling changes.
